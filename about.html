About SolutionHub

SolutionHub is a small, independent how-to website. We write practical guides that solve real problems — the kind you search for at 11 pm when something needs fixing now.

What we do

We publish step-by-step guides for everyday digital tasks: working with PDFs, improving photos and audio, editing video, finding business leads, writing better emails, building a CV, and staying safe online. Each guide is written in our own words, in plain English, and tested against the tool it recommends.

What we do not do

We do not copy content from other websites. We do not promise results we cannot deliver. We do not guarantee earnings, rankings, or outcomes of any kind. When a tool has a limit, we say so in the guide instead of hiding it.

Who runs SolutionHub

SolutionHub is run by Dipesh Karki, an independent maker from Nepal who also builds the free tools our guides recommend: PDF Tools Hub, AI Studio, AI Video Clipper and LeadsForceGen. The guides and the tools are made by the same person, which is why the guides describe the tools honestly — limits included.

How the site is funded

SolutionHub is free to read. In future, it may show advertisements through Google AdSense, clearly marked as ads. Advertising never changes what a guide says, and we never sell a guide placement to a tool.
