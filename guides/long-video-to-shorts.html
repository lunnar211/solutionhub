How to Turn a Long Video Into Shorts and Reels

Turn a long video into vertical captioned clips for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok. Upload your video, let AI find the best moments.

You recorded a 20-minute video. Nobody watches 20-minute videos on their phone — but the best 40 seconds of it would do well as a Short or a Reel. The problem is finding those 40 seconds and cutting them by hand, which can take an hour for every minute of finished clip. AI clipping does the boring part: it finds the moments worth keeping and turns them into vertical videos with captions.

This guide shows you how to turn a long video into Shorts and Reels, what to upload, and how to pick the clips worth posting.

What you need

  • The long video file, saved on your device.
  • A modern browser.
  • A few minutes, plus time to watch the clips before you post them.

One important note before you start: you need to upload your own video file. Pasting a link to someone else's video does not work, and the tool will not download videos from links — that is deliberate, to respect creators' rights. Use a video you recorded or one you have the rights to.

Step 1: Open the clipper and upload your video

Open the AI Video Clipper and upload your video file. Guests can try it without an account. The tool first transcribes your video — it listens to everything said and writes it down with timings — because the words are how it finds the best moments.

Step 2: Let the AI find the highlights

The tool scores the transcribed moments for how interesting, clear, and complete they are, and suggests the strongest clips. A good clip is one complete thought: a tip, a story, a surprising fact, or a clear answer to a question. It should make sense on its own, without the rest of the video. Review the suggestions and pick the ones that stand alone.

Step 3: Check the captions and the crop

Each clip is rendered vertical, with animated captions and the speaker kept in frame. Watch every clip before you post it. Check two things: that the captions match the words (AI transcription is good, not perfect), and that the crop has not cut off anything important. If a caption is wrong, fix the text — a wrong caption is worse than no caption, because viewers read it before they listen.

Step 4: Download and post

Download the clips you like and post them one at a time, not all at once. One clip a day, with a clear title and a few honest hashtags, beats five clips dumped in an hour. Watch which ones get watched to the end, and make more clips like those.

What makes a clip work

  • Start with the point. The first two seconds decide whether anyone stays. Put the surprising or useful part first.
  • One idea per clip. A clip that tries to say three things says nothing clearly.
  • Keep it short. 30 to 60 seconds is the sweet spot for most topics. Longer clips need a genuinely good story.
  • Captions matter. Most people watch without sound at first. If the captions are clear, they stay.

Tips for better clips

  • Record with clipping in mind. Speak in short, complete sentences with small pauses between ideas. The AI will find your moments far more easily.
  • Do not post every suggestion. Three strong clips beat ten average ones. Your audience remembers the best, not the most.
  • Try the topic generator too. If you have no long video, the topic-to-short generator writes a script from your topic and renders a short video — guests get 3 renders a day.
  • Check the audio. If the original audio is noisy, clean it first. A clean voice makes a clip feel professional.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to try?

Yes. Guests can try the clipper without an account, and the topic-to-short generator gives guests 3 renders a day.

Can I paste a YouTube link instead of uploading?

Not right now. YouTube link import is not visitor-usable yet, so upload your own video file. That is the supported way to use the tool today.

How long should my video be?

Any length works, but videos of 5 to 30 minutes give the best results — long enough to contain several good moments, short enough to process quickly.

Can I use clips of other people's videos?

No. Only use videos you recorded yourself or have the rights to use. Clipping someone else's video without permission can get your account or channel in trouble.
