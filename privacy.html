Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. SolutionHub is a simple reading website. We collect almost nothing.

What we collect

We do not run accounts, logins, or sign-ups. You can read every guide without giving us your name, email, or any personal information.

When you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that only to reply to you. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with anyone.

Cookies and analytics

SolutionHub itself sets no tracking cookies. The site is hosted on Vercel, which may collect basic, anonymised server logs (such as pages visited and general location) to keep the site running securely. We do not use that data to identify you.

Third-party tools

Our guides link to free tools we operate (PDF Tools Hub, AI Studio, AI Video Clipper, LeadsForceGen). Those tools have their own privacy policies. When you click a link and use a tool, that tool's policy applies. PDF Tools Hub processes files in your browser; AI Studio and the other tools explain their data handling on their own sites.

Advertising

SolutionHub may show advertisements through Google AdSense in the future. Google may use cookies to show ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through Google's Ad Settings. We will update this page before any advertising starts.

Children

SolutionHub is written for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
